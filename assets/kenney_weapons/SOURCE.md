# Third-party 3D weapon assets

WalkingLab downloads original Kenney models from https://github.com/shorepine/kenney (Kenney CC0 collection).

- Sword: `3d/mini-arena/weapon-sword.glb`
- Axe: `3d/survival/tool-axe.glb`
- Dagger / short knife: `3d/weapon/knife_sharp.glb`

Author: Kenney (https://kenney.nl)
License: CC0 1.0 Universal, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

No hand-built primitive weapon meshes are used. RUN.bat installs the original GLB files and Godot imports them.
