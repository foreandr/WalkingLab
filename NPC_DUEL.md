# Autonomous NPC Duel / BLACK spectator
BLACK is immortal, fully controllable for observing, and is **excluded from targeting and blade damage**. Only flight is equipped for the player. Two independent mortal NPCs (red Quaternius UAL1+UAL2 swordfighter, gold AETHER dagger acrobat) duel in a 56 × 56 × 26 m enclosed arena. Both NPCs have the full AI spell inventory, 5-second flight cap, rolls, evasions, jumping, dodging, aerial acrobatics and imported Kenney CC0 weapons. Repositioning and actions depend on opponent range, attacks, cooldowns and vulnerability, not a scripted choreography. AETHER plays genuine authored Backflip and DodgeRoll; Quaternius plays native Roll, Jump, NinjaJump and sword clips. Sideflips remain unavailable from those asset sets. F5 resets. Player maintains WASD, camera and V flight. Smoke test confirms 2 NPCs spawn and arena colliders load; visual filming still required.
